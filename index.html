01 · the short version

The model is the *easy *part. I build the *pipelines, *evaluation, *fallbacks and *monitoring that decide whether AI still works at *3 *a.m. — for a *national *telecom, a *government *portal, and whatever I'm shipping next.

By day I automate a national telecom network at Makedonski Telekom — provisioning platforms on Kubernetes, forecasting models inside Airflow, and an LLM agent layer that does guided provisioning over 530+ real APIs. When an agent's tool call can reconfigure a router, you learn to take guardrails seriously.

Through August 2026 I was a research intern at the Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts, benchmarking GNNs against pretrained molecular models for drug discovery — work that became ADMET-Bench. Evenings and weekends go to things I run as real deployments: a marketing agent with a four-level LLM fallback chain, a fraud-scoring API with full monitoring — and right now, an ERP system with AI built in.

Activity recognition on ScientISST-MOVE (15 subjects, 8 channels at 500 Hz) and stress detection on two Empatica E4 datasets (35 subjects each, up to 4,540 windows × 149 features). parallel computing07

Random Forest, Parallelized with MPI

Fraud detection where the bottleneck is training time. Parallelized both SMOTE oversampling and tree-building with mpi4py, master–worker scatter/gather, on 250,000 transactions — near-linear scaling up to 12 cores with identical precision, recall and F1.

11.7 min → 1.9 min · ~6× speedup

mpi4pyRandom ForestSMOTE

Can a city harvest electricity from footsteps? Footstep-vibration sensor data across five urban locations: regression for each step's mechanical-energy potential, plus walking-pattern classification. Step dynamics, not foot-traffic volume, decide what a pedestrian zone can yield.
