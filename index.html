The short version

By day I automate a national telecom network at Makedonski Telekom — provisioning platforms on Kubernetes, forecasting models inside Airflow, and an LLM agent layer that does guided provisioning over 530+ real APIs. When an agent's tool call can reconfigure a router, you learn to take guardrails seriously.

Through August 2026 I was a research intern at the Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts, benchmarking GNNs against pretrained molecular models for drug discovery — work that became ADMET-Bench. Evenings and weekends go to side projects I run as real deployments: a marketing agent with a four-level LLM fallback chain, a fraud-scoring API with full monitoring.

The pattern across all of it: the model is the easy part. Data pipelines, evaluation, fallbacks and monitoring are what decide whether it works at 3 a.m. That's the part I'm good at.

llm agents · in production01

Autonomous Marketing Agent

A marketing department in software, for a real gaming-PC retailer. A 7-stage agent pipeline — research, strategy, copy, self-review, repair — writes channel-specific posts across six platforms over a 1,000-product catalog, behind a deterministic policy gate. The interesting part is the fallback chain: primary CLI agent → Groq → self-hosted Ollama (llama.cpp, GGUF) → deterministic template, so generation degrades gracefully instead of failing. Nothing goes public without human approval over Telegram; a rehearsal mode tests real platform calls before publishing.

IEEE Fraud Detection Pipeline

End-to-end fraud scoring on the IEEE-CIS dataset. Optuna-tuned LightGBM/XGBoost took AUC from 0.85 to 0.94, served by a real-time FastAPI scorer at ~146 ms with three-tier ALLOW/REVIEW/BLOCK routing. biosignals · with two FCSE colleagues06

Biosignal ML on Three PhysioNet Datasets

Wearable-sensor ML done properly: activity recognition on ScientISST-MOVE (15 subjects, 8 channels at 500 Hz, 6 activities) and stress detection on two Empatica E4 datasets (35 subjects each, up to 4,540 windows × 149 features). Each dataset got its own pipeline — filtering, windowing, NeuroKit2 features, nested GroupKFold — and they meet only at the final comparison. The headline finding: per-subject normalisation beat every model choice, worth +0.21 macro-F1 on its own.

parallel computing07

Random Forest, Parallelized with MPI

Fraud detection where the bottleneck is training time. Parallelized both SMOTE oversampling and Random Forest tree-building across cores with mpi4py, master–worker scatter/gather. On 250,000 transactions, training dropped from 11.7 minutes to 1.9 minutes — a ~6× speedup with near-linear scaling up to 12 cores and identical precision, recall and F1.

ml · energy08

Smart City Footstep Energy

Can a city harvest electricity from footsteps? ML analysis of footstep-vibration sensor data across five urban locations: regression to estimate each step's mechanical-energy potential, plus classification of walking patterns where Gradient Boosting won at ~70% accuracy. The useful insight: step dynamics, not foot-traffic volume, decide how much energy a pedestrian zone can actually yield.
